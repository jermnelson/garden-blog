# Second Potato Harvest, Wireworms, and Meal
When I got back from my work trip to Prague and visiting my good friend
Stephan and his family in Germany, the potato plants in the first row
in the Northwest garden bed were all dried up. I knew that there had to
be some harvestable potatoes so I started digging up the soil around
those plants:

![Northwest Garden Bed, Second Potato Harvest](img/02026-09-07-nw-bed-harvest-two.png)

Here you can see the harvested red potatoes from the first plant:

![Harvesting Red Potatoes](img/02026-09-07-harvesting-red-potatoes.png)


When harvesting, I noticed some damage from what I later determined were
[wireworms](https://extension.usu.edu/planthealth/research/wireworms/) in 
the soil. Here is damage I saw in one of the larger red potato (and which 
might explain how these potato plants started showing signs of stress about
a month ago):

![Wireworm damage in a Red Potato](img/02026-09-07-wireworm-damage-potato.png)

After harvesting the last of potatoes later this fall, I want to try a couple of
the suggestions for removing these larva worms from the bed as part of a general
prep to improve the garden beds for next year's garden.
