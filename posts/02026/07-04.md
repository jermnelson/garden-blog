# Happy 250th Birthday United States of America
Today festivities for July 4th was relatively minor but this morning, myself, my wife, and 
her friend Ali attended the [Bayfield 4th of July Parade](https://www.durangoherald.com/articles/letters-to-the-editor/dont-forget-bayfield-on-the-fourth/). It was enjoyable and after eating breakfast at the Lion's club,
we watched the parade. 

Reflecting on the anniversary of my country's founding two hundred and fifty years ago, I feel a 
background level of anxiety on the future of our country coupled with pride for the good things
we have accomplished as a nation but sobered by how we failed to live up to our ideals. United 
States of America is an ongoing experiment as we strive for a more prefect union, and how can
we rise the challenges of unequal opportunities for all of our citizens and people, the growing
threats of climate changes, and continued vitriol of our public spaces.
