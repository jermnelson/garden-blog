# Harvesting Potatoes and Onions
For the past three weeks or so, the night temperatures have 
been dropping below freezing and all of the remaining potatoes,
green beans, and onions all wilted. I decided to harvest the 
potatoes and onions (the green bean plants did not have any
beans).
