# Tomato Plant Uprooted, Purslane "Weed", and Butterfly
The past couple of days we've had some thunderstorms with 
heavy rain and one of the tomato plants were uprooted and need
replanting as seen here:

![Tomato Plant Uprooted](img/02026-07-19-uprooted-tomato.png)

I was curious about a common weed I've seen in the garden bed,
so I took this photo and used Apple's AI to identify this 
plant:

![Purslane Weed](img/02026-07-19-purslane.png)

It turns out this is, [Portulaca oleracea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portulaca_oleracea) or
Purslane or pursley, an edible plant that can be eaten raw or cooked! Very 
cool and before I was weeding and putting this plant into the compost, I'm
going to start harvesting this plant instead and trying out some different
recipes. I did taste a couple of leafs and one leaf was a little bitter but
otherwise it has a pretty mild taste raw.
